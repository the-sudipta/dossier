===== Naming Conventions =====

[1] SEMESTER NAMES MEANING
----------------------------------
- FALL 25-26 means Fall 2025
- SPRING 25-26 means Spring 2026
- SUMMER 25-26 means Summer 2026
- FALL 26-27 means Fall 2026 

[2] FILE NAMES
----------------------------------
SEMESTER_NAME.COURSE_NAME_[ SECTION ].TERM_NAME_MID_OR_FINAL.FILE_NAME_OR_DETAILS_LIKE_OBE.FILE_EXTENSION
Example : Fall_25-26.DS_Lab_[ G ].Final_Term.Final_Exam.pdf

[3] COURSE FOLDER NAMES
----------------------------------
COURSE NAME [ SECTION ]
Example : DS Lab [ G ]
